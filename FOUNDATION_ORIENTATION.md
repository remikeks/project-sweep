# Foundations of Social Work — shared orientation

## Purpose and boundary

This is a short, optional pre-catalogue orientation for every SWEEP learner.
It is **not** a School, course, assessment, badge, certificate, or learner
completion record. Its only purpose is to help a learner understand the
social-work lens and choose an appropriate specialist pathway.

The archived Render **Foundation of Social Work** school contained one course,
`FCSW 101: Introduction to Social Work`, with ten modules. That course is not
being migrated as an eleventh School because it would overlap all ten active
Schools and imply a new certificate pathway.

## Curated orientation

| Part | Reuses the useful intent of the archive | Deliberate boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Welcome to SWEEP and intentional learning | platform orientation, personal development planning | no enrolment or progress record |
| The social-work lens | purpose, person-in-environment, values, ethics, roles | no practice instruction, legal guidance, or assessment |
| Choose a pathway and keep growing | fields of practice, professional development, wellbeing | directs learners to active specialist Schools rather than repeating their curriculum |

The historic modules on history, sustainable development, competencies,
innovation, and wellbeing remain archive references. They are not copied into
the orientation because the active catalogue already treats their specialist
dimensions in the relevant Schools.
