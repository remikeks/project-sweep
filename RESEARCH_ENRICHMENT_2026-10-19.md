# Original-course research enrichment — batch 1

## Scope

Six original courses receive one optional reading supplement in their first
online module. Existing module text, completion rules, assessments and all 240
baseline downloadable files are unchanged. No school, course, module or asset
rows are created. This is not enrichment of all 30 original course packs.

The source is `courses/research_readings.json`; presentation is gated by the
existing enrolled/unlocked-module rules. Summaries distinguish reported findings
from limitations. Activities and reflection prompts are SWEEP-authored, use
fictional situations and do not ask learners to upload identifiable cases.

## Duplication and release checks

- Exactly six distinct course codes and six distinct DOI identities.
- Notes appear only in module 1, not repeated across all modules.
- No online research call occurs during a learner page request.
- Tests cover enrolled access, anonymous/non-enrolled restrictions, existing
  lesson preservation and absence of assessment or asset creation.
- Course-code/title mapping was checked against the authoritative baseline
  catalogue before release.
