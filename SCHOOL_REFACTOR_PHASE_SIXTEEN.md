# Phase 16 EIL

The live baseline had 96 active courses, 520 published resources and 25 closed school enrolments. The School of Extractive Industries, Livelihoods and SW-EMHEC (EIL) had zero courses against its target of eight. The eight additions are SWP-097 through SWP-104. They include 24 modules, 24 practice activities and 120 review MCQs checked against 930 earlier question stems.

The courses cover community relations, impact assessment literacy, livelihood restoration and just transition, health referral coordination, land displacement and benefit negotiation, grievance handling, corporate accountability and a practicum. The eight share the fictional Afirin Ridge case setting. Teaching focuses on social work evidence and decisions within role. The school retains the recorded SW-EMHEC name. Its expansion and any distinct competency framework are unconfirmed. EIL applies cross-sector practice. SNC and SPIA retain their separate statutory scope.

The curriculum compares topic breadth with the University of Queensland's Social Impact Assessment in Resources course and University of Toronto's Environment, Social & Governance curriculum. The evidence register records IFC, OHCHR, ILO, EITI, OECD, UNEP and NEITI sources, along with Q1 open research from Nigeria and Ghana and contextual IJSDS scholarship. The notes distinguish international guidance, historical evidence and research limitations. See the [curriculum map](production_courseware/phase_sixteen/eil_batch_01/curriculum_alignment.json) and [evidence register](production_courseware/phase_sixteen/eil_batch_01/research.py).

The draft ParaLearn school exam totals 100 marks. It contains 40 unbound multiple-choice slots, six short answers that collectively cover all eight courses and two essays that each cover all eight. The proposed 210-minute duration and 70 percent pass threshold await piloting. Written responses require human marking and independent moderation. Graded delivery requires verified course and school provider bindings and an unpublished question bank. School certification requires authorised signatures from the Head of Learning and Founder Prof. Mina Ogbanga. The Head of Learning details are pending.

Build with `scripts/build_eil_source.py`, `scripts/build_eil_eight_course_exam.py` and `scripts/build_eil_media.py`. The default Django database connection is live. Run local tests against isolated SQLite. Use the scoped owner approval after verifying rendered PDFs, hashes and the prior records. The academy applies private storage and signed downloads to LMS copies. The repository contains the public learning files and review examples.
