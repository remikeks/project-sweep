# Climate Justice and Resilient Communities review brief

## Draft status

This is an in-review course package. The 2026-10-16 staging verification
recorded an inactive course and eight private Storage resources marked
`in_review`. It is not visible to learners. Its host is the School of Disaster
Response and Crisis Intervention. A local 15-question assessment draft now
exists; it has not been imported into ParaLearn or assigned a remote ID.

## Purpose and overlap boundary

The course adds climate-specific social-work capability that does not appear
as a complete learning sequence in the current catalogue:

- identifying how climate impacts distribute unequally across people and
  communities;
- supporting accessible, community-led adaptation; and
- contributing to accountable climate decisions within the learner's role.

It does not replace or repeat existing courses:

| Existing course | Retained scope | Boundary for this draft |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Disaster Preparedness and Response Planning | general hazards, readiness, evacuation, response coordination | no generic preparedness-plan, evacuation, or incident-coordination instruction |
| Long-Term Disaster Recovery Support | recovery case support, service continuity, post-emergency community recovery | no recovery case-management sequence or disaster-response referral protocol |
| Community Needs Assessment | participatory evidence and prioritisation | does not teach general assessment methods; uses climate inequity only as a focused context |
| Policy Analysis Fundamentals and Legislative Advocacy and Coalition Building | policy analysis and campaigning methods | does not teach legislative process or generic policy-analysis tools; applies accountable community voice to climate decisions |

## Required approval

Before any production release, obtain:

1. a climate-justice or climate-adaptation social-work subject-matter review;
2. accessibility and inclusion review, including disability, language, and
   low-connectivity considerations;
3. safeguarding and local legal review for climate displacement, land,
   livelihood, and referral references; and
4. curriculum-lead approval of the full 10-school, 34-course release and the
   ParaLearn assessment blueprint.
