# Digital Social Work Practice Review Brief

## Status

In review. This is an additive course draft and is not registered in the live
catalogue, uploaded to private Storage, assigned a ParaLearn assessment, or
visible to learners.

## Placement

- School: School of Community & Macro Practice
- Course code: SWP-031
- Course title: Digital Social Work Practice
- Intended position: fourth course in its school

This placement treats digital inclusion and digital service design as a
community-practice capability. The course does not duplicate the existing
Community Needs Assessment, Community Organizing and Coalition Building, or
Program Development and Evaluation courses. It adds the distinct professional
practice areas of digital access, information governance, telepractice, and
human oversight of AI-assisted work.

## Draft learning sequence

1. Digital inclusion and service design
2. Information governance and secure practice
3. Telepractice, AI, and professional accountability

## Included review package

- learner guide in DOCX and PDF;
- practice worksheet in DOCX and PDF;
- three module video-plan transcripts in DOCX; and
- a six-slide PowerPoint deck.

## Required approvals before publication

1. A social-work subject-matter expert must verify learning outcomes,
   terminology, supervision boundaries, and scope-of-practice guidance.
2. A privacy and information-governance reviewer must localise consent,
   records, retention, incident, and secure-communication requirements for
   the delivery jurisdiction.
3. An accessibility reviewer must assess the learning materials and the
   proposed delivery channels, including an equivalent non-digital route.
4. A ParaLearn assessment owner must produce and approve the versioned CBT
   package and assign the authoritative assessment identifier.
5. The curriculum lead must approve the final full 31-course release before
   the course is added to production.

## Production gate after approval

Build the full ten-school catalogue, create the missing course only with the
explicit catalogue command flag, upload the eight checksum-verified resources
to the private course-assets bucket as `in_review`, then use the existing
review and publish workflow. Do not transfer historical completion,
assessment, badge, or certificate records to this course.
