# Leadership Supervision and Programme Management review brief

## Draft status

This is an in-review course package only. It has not been registered in the
database, uploaded to Supabase Storage, assigned a ParaLearn assessment, or
made visible to learners. Its intended home is the School of Community & Macro
Practice.

## Purpose and overlap boundary

The course adds a focused sequence for leading and supporting people who
deliver community programmes. It covers role clarity, reflective supervision,
team support, governance, escalation, and using implementation experience to
make proportionate changes.

It does not replace or repeat existing courses:

| Existing course | Retained scope | Boundary for this draft |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Program Development and Evaluation | needs-to-goals design, theory of change, indicators, and evaluation | does not teach programme design, logic models, indicator selection, or evaluation methods |
| Community Organizing and Coalition Building | mobilisation, coalition formation, shared campaigns, and community power | does not teach community mobilisation, campaign planning, or coalition formation |
| Digital Social Work Practice | digital service access, information governance, telepractice, and AI accountability | addresses supervision generally and does not teach digital-service governance or privacy controls |
| Disaster Preparedness and Response Planning | readiness, operational response, referrals, and staff safety during a disaster | does not teach incident command, evacuation, or disaster response coordination |
| Biopsychosocial Assessment and Crisis Stabilization and Safety Planning | individual assessment, acute risk, and safety planning | does not teach clinical assessment or crisis intervention; it focuses on supervisory escalation within a professional role |

## Required approval

Before any production release, obtain:

1. a leadership and community-programme-management subject-matter review;
2. a social-work supervision and professional-practice review;
3. safeguarding and workforce-wellbeing review, including clear routes for
   addressing concerns that need formal escalation;
4. local legal and organisational review for employment, delegated authority,
   whistleblowing, reporting, and supervision requirements; and
5. curriculum-lead approval of the full 10-school, 33-course release and the
   ParaLearn assessment blueprint.
