# Participatory Research and Practice Innovation review brief

## Draft status

This is an **in-review-only** course package. It is not yet registered in the
learner-visible catalogue, published to Supabase Storage, launched through
ParaLearn, or eligible for a credential. Its proposed home is the School of
Community & Macro Practice.

## Purpose and boundaries

The course adds ethical participatory practice inquiry and a bounded approach
to improving a service with community partners. It does not replace or repeat:

- **Community Needs Assessment**, which profiles needs and sets community
  priorities rather than teaching a general practice-inquiry process.
- **Program Development & Evaluation**, which develops programme theories,
  indicators, and evaluations rather than participatory inquiry methods.
- **Using Data to Drive Policy Change**, which translates evidence into policy
  communication rather than teaching co-research or service-improvement work.
- **Digital Social Work Practice**, which addresses digital inclusion,
  information governance, and AI-enabled practice rather than participatory
  research design.
- **Community Organizing & Coalition Building**, which develops collective
  mobilisation and coalition practice rather than an inquiry method.

## Required approvals

Before activation, obtain and record:

1. review from a participatory-research or practice-innovation subject-matter
   expert;
2. research-ethics, data-protection, and applicable local legal review;
3. accessibility, inclusion, safeguarding, and community-partner review; and
4. curriculum-lead approval of the full ten-school, 34-course, 102-module,
   272-asset release and its aligned ParaLearn assessment.

## Production gate

Use this package only through the review-staging workflow. It may create an
inactive course and in-review assets, but it must not publish a course, launch
a learner assessment, award a credential, alter school certification exams, or
transfer a historical Render learner record. Publication requires the recorded
approvals above and the normal full-release audit.

## Local verification record

The read-only release audit verified one school, one course, three modules, and
eight checksum-verified assets. The two PDFs and six-slide course deck were
rendered and visually reviewed. The bundled workspace does not include
LibreOffice, so its document renderer could not produce page previews for the
five DOCX files; review them in Word or LibreOffice before external release.
