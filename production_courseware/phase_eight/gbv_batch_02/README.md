# GBV completion batch: original review drafts

Evidence review: 15 November 2026. SWP-053 Community Prevention and Social Norms Change; SWP-054 Child Marriage FGM and Harmful Practices; SWP-055 Technology Facilitated Abuse and Digital Safety; SWP-056 Ethical GBV Data and Programme Evaluation.

Four courses, twelve ordered modules and sixty original MCQs mapped to sixty teaching points. Each module has at least 250 teaching words, a learning outcome, an original non-graphic fictional case, an activity, feedback and reference IDs. The compiler checked all 270 earlier question stems. Three proposed study hours per course remain subject to piloting. These are introductory continuing-learning drafts, not a professional social-work degree, specialist clinical/GBV/child-casework qualification or certification of service compliance.

## Scope and curriculum fit

This batch completes the proposed eight-title GBV draft catalogue. The earlier four courses address gender/power, first response, supervised casework planning and trauma-informed coordination. These four extend prevention, harmful practices, digital abuse and ethical evaluation. Inter-agency standards, updated child-marriage guidance, WHO's current prevention frameworks and recent OA syntheses inform this breadth; no source prescribes exactly eight short courses or accredits the academy. Independent reviewers must assess depth, outcomes, supervised skills and hours before approving the school curriculum.

All coursework uses supplied fictional descriptions/aggregate numbers. It never requests real disclosures, survivor files, sensitive images, account credentials or child interviews. Learners may pause or use an alternative fictional exercise. Child-specific best interests, evolving capacities, consent/assent and caregiver safety are distinguished from adult support. Qualified safeguarding, legal, clinical and technical decisions remain outside introductory scope. No Nigerian legal age, reporting rule, treatment procedure, crisis number or real service availability is invented.

Only linked sources and original factual commentary, cases, exercises and questions are provided. No source figures, quotations, manuals, assessment instruments, case forms, safety templates, logos or datasets are reproduced/adapted. Open access does not remove licence conditions or questionnaire rights. Raw downloads, Crossref metadata, failed requests and follow-up retrieval receipts stay in ignored private research storage. Retrieval success alone is not appraisal, and restricted/empty responses are not labelled full text. No access restrictions were bypassed.

Q1 evidence includes SCImago's own provider-indexed 2025 JoGH/PLOS ONE records (direct tables restricted) and JMIR's publisher-reported June 2026 announcement of 2025 JIF Q1 categories. Independent JCR tables were not downloaded. No IJGO, BMJ Public Health or IJSDS quartile is asserted. Journal rank does not establish article validity. The previous IJSDS catalogue review found no verifiable relevant GBV article; no unsupported citation is inserted to meet a quota. Current topical OA research is used where its actual methods support the lesson.
