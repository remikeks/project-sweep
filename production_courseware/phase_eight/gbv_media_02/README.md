Slides recap teaching; guides contain the fuller explanations, original fictional cases, activities, feedback, linked sources and evidence/rights limits. No actual video is supplied; video, transcripts and checked captions follow later.

Use only the supplied non-graphic fictional descriptions and aggregate exercise numbers. Do not seek real disclosures or submit survivor records, passwords, identifying screenshots or intimate images. Learners may pause or use an alternative fictional exercise. Materials do not qualify clinicians, forensic practitioners, legal advisers or specialist GBV/child caseworkers. Qualified roles, applicable procedures, supervision and observed skills remain necessary.

The complete eight-course GBV catalogue is authored in draft across Phases 7 and 8. This media bundle contains Phase 8's four courses only. Course and school exam answer keys, staff rubrics, operational receipts and QA images are excluded from the review ZIP. It is not a complete eight-course learner-release package or an approved exam.
