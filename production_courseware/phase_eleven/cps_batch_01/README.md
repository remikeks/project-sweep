Each addition has three substantive modules and fifteen original point-mapped MCQs. Compilation checks 465 earlier stems plus the new questions. Source and assessment objects remain internal version 0.1.0 draft contracts; they are not configured ParaLearn exams. Owner learning-material approval is recorded separately in releases/cps-2026-11-24/owner_approval.json and does not claim external academic accreditation or professional competence.

## Final school exam

The internal blueprint covers all eight CPS courses: forty unbound MCQ slots, six five-mark short answers collectively covering every course and two fifteen-mark essays each integrating all eight. Total 100 marks; 210 minutes and 70 percent are proposed pending piloting. Original retained domains are read from the existing catalogue. ParaLearn assessment ID is null and mixed-item support unverified. Written responses require human criterion scores and rationales, a separately identified human moderator, rubric version and amendment/appeal history. schools/cps_exam_review.py checks the offline contract; an offline result never authorises a certificate.

Badges require an approved course and verified ParaLearn course pass. A school certificate additionally requires every approved school course, the moderated final mixed exam and authorised signatures from the Head of Learning and Founder Prof. Mina Ogbanga. Head of Learning details/signature remain pending. School enrolment and certification stay closed until those gates are actually ready.
