# CPS Phase 11 learning materials

Owner-authorised learning edition, 24 November 2026. Five courses SWP-066 through SWP-070 each have a learner guide PDF, a three-page practice worksheet PDF, an editable slide deck PPTX and its matching slide PDF. Use the guide for full teaching and evidence limits; slides provide the recap. Guides use embedded 12-point body text. Video lessons are not yet produced.

Exercises use invented cases and aggregate information only. Do not contact children or families, collect personal histories, upload real case records, investigate allegations or deploy through this coursework. An alternative fictional exercise may be used. Introductory learning does not confer statutory, clinical, independent casework or deployment authority.

Course materials are separate from ParaLearn assessment availability. Reading materials does not award a badge or certificate. Badges require verified course passes. School certification also requires all approved school courses, the final mixed ParaLearn exam, human written marking, independent moderation and authorised signatures.
