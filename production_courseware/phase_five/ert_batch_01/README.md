The new courses deepen the retained introduction through different outputs. They do not replace it or copy its lessons. ERT covers community/ecosocial practice. Blue-economy specialisation remains with BLU and organisational environmental sustainability with SUS. Clinical treatment, environmental sampling, engineering certification and regulatory decisions require the relevant qualified and authorised role.

Each new course has three modules, at least 250 teaching words per module, original fictional cases, activities, feedback and cited evidence. Five teaching points per module map to five draft MCQs: sixty questions in this batch. Duplicate checks include the previous 105 authored question stems. Source hashes and structural checks establish traceability, not independent academic approval or psychometric validity.

## Curriculum research and remaining breadth

The [adopted CSWE 2022 EPAS](https://www.cswe.org/sites/default/files/2026-07/2022_EPAS.pdf), especially environmental justice, evidence-informed practice, engagement, assessment, intervention and evaluation domains, inform the map. The [CSWE Environmental Justice guide](https://www.cswe.org/sites/default/files/2026-07/Curricular_Guide_Environmental_Justice_2015_EPAS.pdf) is a **2020 supplementary guide aligned to older 2015 EPAS**, not a new standard. The official standards page still identifies 2022 EPAS as the adopted edition; the 2029 draft is not treated as adopted. No source exercises or assignments are reproduced.

## Exam and recognition

`school_exam_blueprint.json` is an internal authoring map for the **provisional five-course milestone**. Its target remains eight, `curriculum_complete` is false, and the three remaining titles are listed. Before ERT launch, extend and review the exam for **all eight approved curriculum courses**. Do not publish this intermediate blueprint as the final full-school exam.

The draft has forty one-mark MCQ slots, six five-mark short answers and two fifteen-mark essays: 100 marks, with eight MCQs and written coverage per current course. Written items have prompts, marking guidance and criterion rubrics. School MCQs remain unbound; the sixty course MCQs are separate staff-review banks. Duration 180 minutes and pass threshold 70 percent are proposals requiring review, adjustments and piloting. Provider ID is null; ParaLearn capabilities and provisioning remain unverified.

Human marking and moderation are required. AI alone cannot finalise written results, and the offline authoring contract cannot issue a certificate. Course badges require approved course content and verified ParaLearn course-CBT passes. ERT certification requires the entire approved eight-course curriculum, a passed final moderated mixed ParaLearn exam and authorised Head of Learning/Founder signatures. ## Materials, reproduction and staging

Use the four-file `pdf_slides` standard: learner-guide PDF, practice-worksheet PDF, editable PPTX and matching slide PDF. Keep 12-point PDF body text, large editable slide text, course/version identity and **ERT** school labels. Videos and accessible captions/checked transcripts are later additions. Staff packages contain assessment keys; learner media do not. `production_courseware` is excluded from application deployment.

Compile with `scripts/build_course_review_batch.py`, passing this authoring source, output directory and both prior packages. Compile the milestone exam with `scripts/build_ert_five_course_exam.py`. Reuse the media builders with this package and a new release path. Render the actual final PPTX through LibreOffice and inspect every final PDF page through Poppler before finalising the manifest.

Create-only staging uses `stage_authored_review`, then `stage_pdf_slide_resources`, with fresh receipts `private/phase-five/courses.json` and `resources.json`. Resources enter private Storage and `in_review`, never academic approval. School identities are checked against the actual course before Storage access. Use the generic read-only `scripts/verify_authored_media_phase.py` with this phase's expected counts; older verifiers intentionally represent older catalogue totals.
