# ERT foundation PDF and slide review materials

Four inactive draft courses, SWP-042 through SWP-045. Each has a seven-page learner-guide PDF, three-page practice worksheet, sixteen-slide editable PPTX and matching sixteen-page slide PDF. The sixteen resources contain sixty-four editable slides and 104 final PDF pages.

Version 0.1.0, English, draft for academic review. Independent academic, Nigerian practice/legal and accessibility/pilot approvals remain pending. ERT reaches five catalogue courses including its unchanged existing introduction; its full target is eight, so this is an intermediate breadth milestone. These materials do not enable a badge, school exam or certificate.

Guides use a 12-point body font, clear headings and course/version page furniture. Slides use the established SWEEP Academy cover background and navy/green/white styling, large editable teaching text and the correct ERT school label. Fuller teaching and evidence limits are in the guides and speaker notes. Worksheets support original fictional practice tasks. Course CBT keys and school-exam marking guidance are excluded from learner media.

All 104 final PDF pages were rendered with Poppler and visually inspected, including the slide PDFs exported from the actual final PPTX through isolated LibreOffice. No clipping or overlap was found. The four PPTX files passed package, geometry, font and Artifact Tool import checks. Native Microsoft PowerPoint was not used. Source checks verified every teaching paragraph, case, activity, feedback and module title in the guides, and all fifteen teaching points per course in the editable decks and slide PDFs. A paragraph crossing a page boundary is checked after removal of the exact header/footer text, without weakening the required source comparison.

`upload_manifest.json` binds the final sixteen files and their intended `in_review` status with SHA-256 checksums. `package_validation.json` records the source-package digest and final-file checks. Source and reference records are in `../ert_batch_01/`. Private QA images, candidate decks, credentials and operational receipts stay out of Git. Production QA is distinct from academic or accessibility approval.

The standard package is PDF and slides until actual videos are produced. Later video resources need accessible captions or a checked transcript; notes are not presented as recorded videos. Private staging preserves all previous course, module, CBT, resource and learner records. Use the current phase verifier for remote hash and retained-record checks.
