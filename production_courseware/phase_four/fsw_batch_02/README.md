# FSW second development batch

Draft for academic, Nigerian practice/legal and accessibility/pilot review. Evidence checked on 14 November 2026. No approval, video, provider assessment or credential is implied.

## Curriculum coverage

FSW reaches its proposed ten-course catalogue through three retained health-application courses and seven new foundational drafts. The original courses and CBT mappings remain unchanged. Ten courses is the agreed short-course school target, not equivalence to a professional degree, supervised placement or registration.

The [IFSW/IASSW education standards](https://www.ifsw.org/global-standards-for-social-work-education-and-training/) inform selected context, practice, ethics and reflective-learning domains. These are introductory courses. They do not replace the standards' wider educational, field-learning, institutional and professional requirements. Detailed review of depth and instructional hours remains necessary before approval.

Scope distinctions are explicit: SWP-036 manages a person's support plan; SWP-040 manages shared cross-sector referral arrangements. SWP-018 concerns healthcare teams; SWP-040 spans sectors. SWP-035 teaches ethical decision processes; SWP-041 applies reflection and authorised supervision to practice learning. Institutional leadership belongs to LMI, rather than being claimed by this introductory supervision course.

Each new course has three modules, at least 250 teaching words per module, an outcome, original fictional case, activity, feedback and cited evidence. Each module has five teaching points mapped to five MCQs. The five courses contain 75 draft MCQs. Structural checks and source hashes establish traceability, not independent academic review, psychometric quality or learner effectiveness. The previous 30 question stems are included in duplicate checks.

## Assessment and release boundary

`school_exam_blueprint.json` supersedes the earlier five-course proposal for future review without rewriting its historical file. It identifies all ten required courses: four MCQ slots per course plus written coverage for every course. Six short answers at five marks and two essays at fifteen marks join the forty one-mark MCQs for 100 marks. The 70 percent threshold and 180-minute duration are proposals requiring review and piloting. Prompts, model marking guidance and rubrics are supplied for all written items. The forty school MCQ slots remain unbound pending reviewed item selection and provider verification; the 75 course MCQs are separate review banks.

The provider remains ParaLearn CBT, with no verified school assessment ID. Human marking and moderation are required for written answers; AI alone cannot finalise a result. The blueprint and offline acceptance contract cannot issue a certificate. School enrolment and certification remain closed. A future certificate requires all approved course passes, a passed final moderated school exam, the Head of Learning's name and authorised signature, and the authorised Founder signature of Prof. Mina Ogbanga.

`review_package.json` and the blueprint include assessment authoring material for staff review. They are excluded from application deployment by `.vercelignore`. Learner PDFs/PPTX contain teaching, practice feedback and references, never CBT answer keys. The twenty resources use the same design and four-file PDF/slides standard as phase three. Actual videos and accessible captions/checked transcripts are later additions.

## Reproduction

Run `scripts/build_course_review_batch.py --authoring production_courseware/phase_four/fsw_batch_02/authoring.py --output production_courseware/phase_four/fsw_batch_02 --previous-package production_courseware/phase_two/fsw_batch_01/review_package.json`, then `scripts/build_fsw_ten_course_exam.py`.

The media builders accept `--package` and `--release`; the deck builder takes release, optional course filter and package path as positional arguments. Their defaults preserve the previous batch paths. Use fresh output directories for revised decks; never rebuild and overwrite already-staged media. Render the actual final PPTX through LibreOffice, then render and inspect every final PDF page with Poppler. Finalize hashes and the upload manifest only after QA.

Use create-only `stage_authored_review` followed by `stage_pdf_slide_resources` with new private receipt paths. Verify the latest phase boundary using `scripts/verify_fsw_phase_four.py`; prior phase verifiers represent earlier counts and should not be rerun as current-state assertions after additive growth.
