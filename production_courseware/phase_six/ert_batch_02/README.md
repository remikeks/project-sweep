## Curriculum scope and evidence

The adopted [CSWE 2022 EPAS](https://www.cswe.org/sites/default/files/2026-07/2022_EPAS.pdf) and the preceding batch's supplementary environmental-justice guide inform selected engagement, assessment, justice, intervention and evaluation domains. Dedicated displacement/livelihood, stewardship/governance and evaluation outputs deepen the introductory content. The eight-course count is the programme's proposed breadth decision, not a claim that the benchmark prescribes eight short courses or that instructional depth and practice competence are independently approved. Independent academic, Nigerian practice/legal and accessibility/pilot review remain pending. BLU's blue-economy specialisation and SUS's organisational sustainability scope stay distinct.

Each of the nine new modules has at least 250 teaching words, a learning outcome, original fictional case, activity, feedback and cited evidence. Five teaching points per module map to five original draft MCQs: 45 questions, checked against all 165 prior authored stems. Canonical source/catalogue digests support traceability. Fifteen questions and three proposed hours per course require academic and learner-pilot review; structural validation alone does not establish depth or psychometric quality.
