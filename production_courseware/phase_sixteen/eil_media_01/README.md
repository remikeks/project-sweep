# EIL learning materials

Eight SWEEP Academy courses, SWP-097 through SWP-104. Each has a learner-guide PDF, a three-page practice worksheet PDF, an editable PowerPoint deck and its matching slide PDF. Guides use 12-point body text. Videos are planned for a later phase.

Read the guide, work through the fictional Afirin Ridge cases, and check your reasoning against the feedback. Use the supplied records and figures for the activities. Refer real clinical, technical, site and legal decisions to the appropriate qualified teams.

Course badges require a verified course CBT pass. School certification requires completion of all eight courses, the final mixed ParaLearn exam, human marking and independent moderation of written answers, and authorised signatures from the Head of Learning and Founder Prof. Mina Ogbanga. Public source questions are review examples. Graded delivery uses a separate unpublished bank.
