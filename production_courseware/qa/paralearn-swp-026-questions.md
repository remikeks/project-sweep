## 1. According to Module 1, which of the following statements accurately defines Psychological First Aid (PFA)?

- A. A formal psychotherapeutic technique aimed at conducting root-cause psychoanalysis of trauma
- B. A humane, supportive, and practical response to a fellow human being who is suffering and may need support **[Correct]**
- C. A psychiatric screening procedure used to formally diagnose Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD)
- D. A mandatory psychological debriefing where survivors must detail their traumatic experiences

Rationale: Module 1 defines PFA as a humane, supportive, and practical first response focused on safety, dignity, and basic support, not formal therapy or clinical diagnosis.

## 2. What does the principle of 'avoiding forced disclosure' in Module 1 require of a PFA practitioner?

- A. Forbidding survivors from talking about their feelings under any circumstance
- B. Requiring written legal consent before allowing a survivor to speak about their experiences
- C. Never pressuring, demanding, or probing a person to tell their story or recount details of what happened **[Correct]**
- D. Insisting that survivors disclose medical history before receiving emergency food or shelter

Rationale: Module 1 stresses that forcing people to talk about their trauma can cause secondary retraumatization; helpers must listen if the person wishes to talk, but never pressure them.
