About SWEEP

The SWEEP Ecosystem brings together Sweep Academy and SweepCare AI to create a single digital platform that develops people, strengthens organizations, and promotes healthier communities. Whether you're a student, professional, organization, government agency, or community leader, our AI-powered solutions help you build skills, improve wellbeing, and connect with the support you need to thrive.

🌱

SWEEP Academy

Sweep Academy is an AI-powered learning and workforce development platform designed to equip individuals and organizations with the knowledge, skills, and professional certifications needed for the future of work and social impact. Through practical courses, expert-led training, digital resources, and professional communities, we help learners build careers, strengthen leadership, and create meaningful change. Build Skills. Earn Certifications. Advance Your Career.

✦
🤝

SweepCare AI

SweepCare AI is an AI-powered preventive mental health and wellbeing platform that helps people understand their wellbeing early and connects them to trusted support before challenges become crises. We do not diagnose or replace healthcare professionals. Instead, we provide confidential wellbeing assessments, personalized insights, trusted referral pathways, and wellbeing resources that empower individuals while helping organizations create healthier, more productive environments. Understand Early. Connect to Support. Thrive.


One Ecosystem. Unlimited Impact.

Together, Sweep Academy and SweepCare AI are building the digital infrastructure for learning, workforce development, and preventive wellbeing—empowering individuals, organizations, and communities to achieve lasting impact through innovation, knowledge, and human-centered AI. Empowering People. Transforming Practice. Improving Wellbeing.
