Why SWEEP exists

Mission & Vision

🤝

Our Mission

To make practice-ready social work training accessible to anyone called to the field — self-paced, evidence-informed, and free of the paywalls that keep vital skills locked away.

✦
🌱

Our Vision

A future where every social worker — wherever they trained and however they entered the field — carries the same foundation of skill, ethics, and confidence to show up for the people who need them most.

Strategic Objectives

These are the objectives that SWEEP aims to meet:

01

Deliver Quality Education

Provide accessible, high-quality, competency-based education and professional certification programmes for social work students and practitioners.

02

Advance Professional Development

Promote lifelong learning through continuing professional development (CPD), specialist training, mentoring, and professional networking.

03

Enhance Employability

Equip graduates and practitioners with practical, digital, leadership, and workplace skills that improve career readiness and employment opportunities.

04

Strengthen Research and Knowledge Exchange

Support research, publications, evidence-based practice, policy development, and knowledge sharing that advance the social work profession.

05

Leverage Digital Technology

Harness digital learning platforms, artificial intelligence, and emerging technologies to improve education, professional practice, and service delivery.

06

Build Strategic Partnerships

Collaborate with universities, professional bodies, governments, NGOs, development partners, and the private sector to expand learning and career opportunities.

07

Promote Ethical and Inclusive Practive

Champion ethical standards, human rights, social justice, diversity, inclusion, and environmental sustainability in social work education and practice.

08

Develop Future Leaders

Build the leadership capacity of social workers to influence policy, strengthen institutions, and lead transformative social change.

09

CReate Sustainable Impact

Establish a financially sustainable academy that delivers measurable social, economic, and professional value to individuals, institutions, and communities.

Our Value Proposition

SWEEP is Africa's first integrated Social Work Education, Enterprise and Professional Development Academy that equips professionals with industry-relevant knowledge, practical skills, globally recognised certifications, career opportunities, entrepreneurship support, and lifelong learning—all in one digital platform. As an institution, we offer a unique value proposition that combines quality education, professional development, and career advancement opportunities for our members. Our platform is designed to empower social workers to excel in their careers, make a positive impact in their communities, and contribute to the growth and development of the social work profession in Africa.
