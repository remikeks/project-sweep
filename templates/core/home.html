Why SWEEP exists

Mission & Vision

🤝

Our Mission

To make practice-ready social work training accessible to anyone called to the field — self-paced, evidence-informed, and free of the paywalls that keep vital skills locked away.

✦
🌱

Our Vision

A future where every social worker — wherever they trained and however they entered the field — carries the same foundation of skill, ethics, and confidence to show up for the people who need them most.

Where we're headed

Strategic Objectives

Placeholder goals for now — these will be refined as the program matures.

01

Full curriculum coverage

Expand and refine course offerings across all 10 schools until every core social work specialty has a complete, practice-ready pathway.

02

Recognized certification

Pursue alignment with professional bodies and continuing-education requirements so SWEEP certificates carry real currency in the field.

03

Equitable access

Keep cost and scheduling from ever being the reason a capable learner can't complete their training.

04

Learner success

Sustain high completion and pass rates through continuous review of course content, assessments, and learner feedback.

05

Community partnerships

Build relationships with agencies, universities, and practicing social workers to keep content grounded in real practice.

06

Platform reliability

Invest in the infrastructure, security, and support behind SWEEP so it stays dependable for learners at every stage.

What sets us apart

Our Value Proposition
