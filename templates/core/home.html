Why SWEEP exists

Mission & Vision

🤝

Our Mission

To bridge the gap between social work educationand practice through digital innovation and entrepreneurship, creating sustainable employmentpathways and cultivating impact-driven leaders across Africa.

✦
🌱

Our Vision

A world where every social worker is empowered, employable, an equipped to transform lives and communities.

Strategic Objectives

These are the objectives that SWEEP aims to meet:

01

Establish Category Leadership

Position SWEEP Academy as the definitive authority and primary hub for digital social work education and professional development in Africa.

02

Scale Pan-African Footprint

Expand influence beyond Nigeria, establishing strategic educational and institutional partnerships in at least three major African economic regions.

03

Drive Workforce Transformation

Close the skill-to-impact gap by ensuring a measurable, high-percentage increasein the employability of graduates.

04

Foster Innovation

Create a sustainable ecosystem for social work entrepreneurship by integrating incubation support that enables professionals to scale community-based solutions.

05

Advocate for Professional Standardisation

Influence regional and continental policy to improve the regulation, recognition, and continuous professional development standards for the social workforce.

Our Value Proposition

SWEEP does not only educate; it builds Africa's social impact workforce. Through AI-enabled learning, \ professional certification, career pathways, and incubator support, SWEEP transforms graduates into employable professionals, successful social entrepreneurs, and community change leaders.
