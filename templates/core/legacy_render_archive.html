What happens to earlier learning records

Past completions, assessment attempts, badges, and certificates remain historical records. They are not automatically transferred into a different current course, School, or certificate path.

If you need help locating a historical record, contact SWEEP Academy with the learner name, approximate date, and the record you are trying to verify.
